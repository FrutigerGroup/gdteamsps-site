Community Rules

Our goal is a safe, fair, and fun server for creators and players.

Core rules

  • Respect everyone. No harassment, hate speech, or targeted insults.
  • No cheating/exploits. Using accounts, clients, or tools to cheat is strictly forbidden.
  • Don’t share private data. No doxxing or leaking private info.
  • No advertising/spam. Keep unsolicited advertising and repeated links out of chat.
  • Appropriate content only. Levels and chat must follow community standards and be age-appropriate.
  • Single account rule. Don’t create alt accounts to evade punishments or manipulate votes.

Enforcement

Violations may lead to warnings, temporary suspensions, or permanent bans depending on severity. Staff decisions can be appealed; use the official contact method and provide evidence.
