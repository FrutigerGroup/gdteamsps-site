Server Rules — GDTEAMSPS

Read these carefully. Breaking rules can result in warnings, kicks, or bans.

  1. Be respectful. No harassment, bullying, slurs, or threats. Respect staff decisions.
  2. No cheating or exploiting. Hacks, trainers, speed mods, or any exploit that gives unfair advantage is prohibited.
  3. No doxxing or sharing personal information. Do not post other players' personal data.
  4. No spamming or advertising. Repeated messages, unsolicited links, or adverts are not allowed.
  5. Keep content appropriate. Levels, names, chat and avatars must be safe for all ages—no pornographic or extreme content.
  6. One account per player. Multi-accounting to bypass bans or exploit systems is forbidden.
  7. Follow staff instructions. If a moderator asks you to stop, comply and appeal later if needed.
  8. Ban appeals. Banned users may file an appeal via the official method (contact page or email) — provide evidence and be clear.
  9. Age & legal. You must follow local laws. If you are under your country's required age, get parental permission for account creation.

Enforcement & Reporting

Staff will warn, mute, kick, or ban depending on severity. To report abuse, contact a moderator with timestamps, screenshots, or replay info.
